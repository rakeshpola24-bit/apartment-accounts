Palm Beach Apartments - monthly sheets

1. Month and costs

The cost shown (5058) is only the September figure. Type the real cost before you print.

2. Meter readings

First column = earlier month, second = later month. Type 8 digits (with the leading zero). Difference shows on the right; red means a reading went down or is 0.

FlatEarlierLaterDiff

Press this after printing. The later readings become next month's earlier readings, saved on this phone/computer. Next month you only type the new readings. (Saved in this browser only; use the same device and browser each time.)

Read readings from a photo (optional)

Take a straight, well-lit photo of the handwritten page, and crop it to the table only (flat numbers and readings, no names). The photo is sent to Google Gemini (free tier), which may use it to improve Google products. You always check the suggestions before they are used.

3. Other charges (same for every flat)

Water rate = total water cost / total units of all flats. Water amount = units x rate, rounded, not below the minimum. Common area meter is shown only for information (fixed rate 0.010519, not in the total).

4. Print

On iPhone: tap Print, then pinch out on the preview (or Share, Save to Files) to get the PDF.
